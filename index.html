A bit about me

Service Designer working across CX and UX

Currently based in Wellington, working on service design and customer experience projects. Most of my day involves mapping services, interviewing people, running workshops, and trying to help teams make things less complicated.

I have ADHD, which means my brain works a bit differently. Good days: I spot patterns others miss and hyperfocus on solving problems. Challenging days: I need to work in ways that actually suit how my brain operates. It's part of who I am.

I think the best work happens when you actually listen to people, stay curious about why things are the way they are, and don't pretend to have all the answers upfront.
